\begin{abstract}
Rung 2's MLP reads its context through a fixed, position-rigid window.
This rung derives the mechanism that removes the rigidity ---
scaled dot-product attention, a data-dependent, differentiable
dictionary lookup --- and hand-verifies it before using it:
\texttt{attention\_theoretical.py} computes the forward \emph{and}
backward passes in raw numpy on a small example and matches torch
autograd to $10^{-16}$ on every tensor, proves causality end to end,
and checks the $\sqrt{d}$ scaling claim by Monte Carlo.
\texttt{attention\_practical\_pytorch.py} then builds the smallest
language model around one transformer block, beats rung 2 on the
shared validation slice, and animates the attention map learning where
to look on real text.
\end{abstract}

\section{The mechanism}

For a sequence $X \in \mathbb{R}^{T \times d}$, three learned linear
maps give each position three roles:
\begin{equation}
  Q = X W_q \ \ (\text{``what am I looking for?''}), \quad
  K = X W_k \ \ (\text{``what do I contain?''}), \quad
  V = X W_v \ \ (\text{``what do I offer?''}),
  \label{eq:qkv}
\end{equation}
each in $\mathbb{R}^{T \times d_h}$. Scores, causal mask, and mixing:
\begin{equation}
  S = \frac{Q K^{\!\top}}{\sqrt{d_h}}, \qquad
  S_{ij} \leftarrow -\infty \ \text{ for } j > i, \qquad
  A = \smax_{\text{rows}}(S), \qquad
  O = A\,V.
  \label{eq:attn}
\end{equation}
Row $i$ of $A$ is a \emph{probability distribution over positions
$\le i$}: position $i$ mixes the values of the positions it chooses to
attend to. Two facts carry the whole design. First, $A$ is
\textbf{not a parameter} --- it is recomputed from the data every
forward pass, so ``which past positions matter'' is itself a learned,
input-dependent computation (this is exactly what rung 2's fixed
window could not do). Second, the mask makes the model causal:
output $i$ depends only on inputs $\le i$, verified below by direct
perturbation.

\begin{remark}[Why $\sqrt{d_h}$]
For $q, k$ with i.i.d.\ unit-variance entries,
$\mathrm{Var}(q \cdot k) = d_h$; unscaled scores of size
$\sqrt{d_h}$ saturate the softmax at initialization and kill its
gradient. Dividing by $\sqrt{d_h}$ restores $O(1)$ scores. Measured
($d_h = 64$, $2\times10^4$ samples): raw variance $65.3 \approx d_h$,
scaled $1.02 \approx 1$.
\end{remark}

\section{The backward pass, by hand}

With upstream gradient $dO$, the chain rule through \eqref{eq:attn}:
\begin{equation}
  \boxed{\;
  \begin{aligned}
  dV &= A^{\!\top} dO, \qquad dA = dO\, V^{\!\top}\\
  dS &= A \odot \bigl(dA - \operatorname{rowsum}(dA \odot A)\bigr)
     && \text{(softmax Jacobian, per row)}\\
  dQ &= dS\, K / \sqrt{d_h}, \qquad dK = dS^{\!\top} Q / \sqrt{d_h}\\
  dW_q &= X^{\!\top} dQ, \quad dW_k = X^{\!\top} dK, \quad
     dW_v = X^{\!\top} dV\\
  dX &= dQ\, W_q^{\!\top} + dK\, W_k^{\!\top} + dV\, W_v^{\!\top}
  \end{aligned}\;}
  \label{eq:backward}
\end{equation}
The masked entries need no special case: they have $A_{ij} = 0$, and
the $A \odot (\cdot)$ factor zeroes their gradient automatically.

\paragraph{Proof by computation.} On a $T{=}6$, $d{=}8$, $d_h{=}4$
example in \texttt{float64}, the numpy implementation of
\eqref{eq:attn}--\eqref{eq:backward} matches torch autograd on
\emph{every} tensor: $O$ and $A$ to $6\times10^{-16}$, and $dX, dW_q,
dW_k, dW_v$ to at most $8.9\times10^{-16}$. Causality is checked
physically: adding $10$ to the last input row changes only the last
output row. Attention is earned; from here it may be used batched and
multi-headed.

\section{One block: communicate, then compute}

Multi-head attention runs $n_h$ copies of \eqref{eq:attn} on
$d/n_h$-dimensional slices and concatenates --- different heads can
track different things (recency, line starts, speaker turns). The
transformer block wraps it with the two stabilizers:
\begin{equation}
  x \leftarrow x + \mathrm{MHA}(\mathrm{LN}(x)), \qquad
  x \leftarrow x + \mathrm{MLP}(\mathrm{LN}(x)),
  \label{eq:block}
\end{equation}
pre-norm residuals: each sublayer learns a \emph{correction} to the
identity, gradients flow through the skip path, and LayerNorm keeps
activations $O(1)$ (the $\sqrt{d_h}$ argument, applied everywhere).
The rung's model is the minimal wrap: token + position embeddings, ONE
block \eqref{eq:block}, a linear head; $T = 64$, $d = 128$, $4$ heads,
$223{,}425$ parameters.

\section{Experiment: the rung is climbed}

AdamW, batch 64, step decay ($10^{-3}$ for 12k steps, $10^{-4}$ for
4k): $16{,}000$ steps in $68$ seconds on the RTX 4070.
\begin{equation}
  \boxed{\;\mathrm{NLL}_{\mathrm{val}} = 1.6254 \ \text{nats/char},
  \qquad \mathrm{PPL} = 5.08\;}
  \label{eq:result}
\end{equation}
against rung 2's $1.7583$ (PPL $5.80$) on the same slice --- with
$8\times$ the context of the MLP and no per-position parameters.
(A first attempt with $d = 96$ and 8k constant-lr steps scored
$1.8252$ and \emph{failed} the gate --- the fix was width + training
time + the lr drop, a preview of rung 4's scaling discussion.)
Samples now hold the play's macro-structure: capitalized speaker names
with colons, dialogue turns, stage-direction rhythm.

The animation shows the mechanism learning: on a fixed validation
window, the head-averaged $A$ starts as a diffuse causal smear and
develops structure as the loss falls --- a strong recency band,
columns lighting up under spaces and newlines (word and line
boundaries are what a character model needs most), heads
specializing --- beside the loss crossing rung 2's line and the
samples sharpening.

\begin{remark}[What still fails --- rung 4's opening]
One block is one round of communicate-then-compute: information hops
a single attention step. Long-range grammar (an open quote closed 40
characters later, a subject agreeing with a distant verb) wants
\emph{iterated} mixing. Rung 4 stacks the block \eqref{eq:block} $N$
deep --- and that is the entire remaining distance to GPT.
\end{remark}
