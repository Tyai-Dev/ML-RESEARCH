\begin{abstract}
The final rung contains \emph{no new mathematics} --- that is its
point. A GPT is the rung-3 block stacked $N$ deep over rung-2's
embeddings, trained on rung-1's loss; every mechanism inside it was
derived by hand and verified against autograd at $10^{-16}$ lower on
the ladder. What this rung adds is engineering with receipts: the
training knobs (AdamW, warmup+cosine, gradient clipping), each
justified by measurement (\texttt{gpt\_ablations.py}) rather than
folklore. The model --- 4 layers, 4 heads, $d = 256$, $T = 128$,
$3.21$M parameters --- trains in $2.3$ minutes on the RTX 4070 to
validation perplexity $\mathbf{4.36}$, beating every rung below it on
the shared slice.
\end{abstract}

\section{The architecture: depth is iterated mixing}

\begin{equation}
  x^{(0)} = \mathrm{TokEmb}[c] + \mathrm{PosEmb}[0..T{-}1],
  \qquad
  x^{(\ell+1)} = \mathrm{Block}(x^{(\ell)}),
  \qquad
  Z = \mathrm{LN}(x^{(N)})\, W_{\mathrm{tok}}^{\!\top},
  \label{eq:gpt}
\end{equation}
with $\mathrm{Block}$ exactly rung 3's
$x \mapsto x + \mathrm{MHA}(\mathrm{LN}(x))$,
$x \mapsto x + \mathrm{MLP}(\mathrm{LN}(x))$. One block is one hop of
information between positions; $N$ blocks iterate the mixing, which is
what long-range structure (a quote that closes, a subject agreeing
with a distant verb) requires --- and the measured NLL drop from one
block to four ($1.63 \to 1.47$) is the largest on the ladder.

Assembly choices, with reasons: the head is \emph{tied} to the token
embedding ($W_{\mathrm{tok}}^{\!\top}$ in \eqref{eq:gpt}) --- the maps
char$\to$vector and vector$\to$char share structure, saving $Vd$
parameters; residual-path projections are initialized with std
$0.02/\sqrt{2N}$ so the residual stream's variance stays $O(1)$ after
its $2N$ additions (the $\sqrt{d_h}$ argument, applied to depth);
dropout ($0.1$) enters the ladder here because $3.2$M parameters
against $10^6$ characters is the first genuinely overparameterized
model --- and indeed training NLL reaches $1.08$ while validation
plateaus at $1.47$: the generalization gap, visible at last.

\section{The knobs, with receipts}

The training loop is still the same $\sim$30 lines; three knobs are
added, each with its mechanism stated and its effect measured.

\paragraph{AdamW.} Adam with \emph{decoupled} weight decay,
$\theta \leftarrow \theta - \eta\lambda\theta$ applied outside the
moment machinery (Theory/optimizers), $\lambda = 0.1$ on matmul
weights only --- biases, LayerNorm gains, and embeddings stay free.

\paragraph{Warmup + cosine.} Adam's second-moment estimates are noise
for the first steps; the schedule
\begin{equation}
  \eta(t) =
  \begin{cases}
    \eta_{\max}\, t/t_w, & t < t_w\\[2pt]
    \eta_{\min} + (\eta_{\max}-\eta_{\min})\,
    \tfrac12\bigl(1 + \cos(\pi u)\bigr), & u = \tfrac{t - t_w}{T - t_w}
  \end{cases}
  \label{eq:cosine}
\end{equation}
($t_w = 300$, $\eta_{\max} = 6\times10^{-4}$,
$\eta_{\min} = 6\times10^{-5}$) protects the early steps and anneals
the endgame, replacing rungs 2--3's crude step-drops.

\paragraph{Gradient clipping.} Rescale the global gradient norm to
$\le 1$: one outlier minibatch cannot throw the parameters.

\paragraph{The ablation's verdict --- measurement over folklore.}
Three recipes (no knobs / +schedule / full), same seed and data order,
$2500$ steps each: final validation NLL $1.5119$ / $1.5058$ / $1.5115$
--- within $0.006$ nats of each other. At this scale the knobs are
\emph{insurance}, not magic: warmup guards an early blow-up this small
stable model doesn't suffer; clipping caps outlier batches this clean
corpus doesn't produce; decay fights an overfit that needs a longer
run to matter. They earn their keep at scale; here they must merely do
no harm, and that is what the assert checks. The repo's rule survives
its first negative result: \emph{improvements are experiments, and a
null result is a result}.

\section{Experiment: the ladder, closed}

$6000$ steps, batch $64$, $2.3$ minutes on the RTX 4070:
\begin{equation}
  \boxed{\;\mathrm{NLL}_{\mathrm{val}} = 1.4724 \ \text{nats/char},
  \qquad \mathrm{PPL} = 4.36\;}
  \label{eq:result}
\end{equation}

Every rung beat the one below it on the same validation slice, each
gate an assert. The samples close the story the bigram opened: from
letter-texture (rung 1), to English words (rung 2), to speaker
structure (rung 3), to near-grammatical blank verse with clause
structure (rung 4): ``\texttt{Unless my friends be unknown with the
cause; / I will follow the blows}\ldots''. The animation plays the
finale: the loss descending through every previous rung's line, the
perplexity ladder as a live bar chart, and the fixed-seed samples
evolving from noise to verse.

\begin{remark}[What the ladder teaches]
The loss never changed. From rung 1's count table to rung 4's GPT,
every model minimized the same average NLL
$-\frac1T\sum\log p(c_{i+1}\mid \mathrm{context})$; the entire journey
was about \emph{which family of conditional distributions} we let the
optimizer search. Language modeling is maximum likelihood --- the rest
is architecture.
\end{remark}
