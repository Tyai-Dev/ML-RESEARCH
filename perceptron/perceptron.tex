\begin{abstract}
Everything before this note assumed a conditional distribution and
maximized its likelihood. The perceptron assumes \emph{nothing}: no
$p(x)$, no noise model, not even that examples are random --- an
adversary may choose them. What replaces the statistical guarantees is a
\emph{mistake bound}: on any stream separable with margin $\gamma$ inside
radius $R$, the perceptron makes at most $(R/\gamma)^2$ mistakes, ever.
We state the algorithm (binary and multiclass), prove Novikoff's theorem
in full, and demonstrate bound, convergence, and the non-separable
failure mode in \texttt{perceptron.py}.
\end{abstract}

\section{The online, distribution-free setting}

Labels $y \in \{-1,+1\}$; examples $(x_t, y_t)$ arrive one at a time, in
any order, chosen by nature or an adversary. The learner maintains a
linear predictor $w$ and pays for every \emph{mistake}
($y_t \ne \operatorname{sign}(w^\top x_t)$). There is no distribution to
be consistent for and no risk to converge to; the object of analysis is
the cumulative mistake count. This is the learning-theory note's
worst-case spirit taken all the way: not worst distribution ---
\emph{worst sequence}.

\section{The algorithm}

Initialize $w_1 = 0$. At step $t$, predict
$\hat y_t = \operatorname{sign}(w_t^\top x_t)$; if
$y_t\, w_t^\top x_t \le 0$ (a mistake), update
\begin{equation}
  w_{t+1} \;=\; w_t + y_t\, x_t ,
  \label{eq:update}
\end{equation}
otherwise leave $w$ unchanged. The update is pure error correction:
after it, $y_t\, w_{t+1}^\top x_t = y_t w_t^\top x_t + \lVert
x_t\rVert^2$ --- the offending example's margin moves up by
$\lVert x_t\rVert^2$. (It is also SGD with step 1 on a perceptron loss
$\max(0, -y\,w^\top x)$; the losses note places it in that family.)

\section{The mistake bound}

\begin{theorem}[Novikoff]
Suppose some unit vector $u$ separates the stream with margin $\gamma$:
$y_t\, u^\top x_t \ge \gamma$ for all $t$, and $\lVert x_t\rVert \le R$.
Then the perceptron makes at most
\begin{equation}
  \boxed{\;M \;\le\; \Bigl(\frac{R}{\gamma}\Bigr)^{2}\;}
  \label{eq:novikoff}
\end{equation}
mistakes on the stream, regardless of its length or order.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Only mistake steps change $w$; let $M$ be their number and consider $w$
after the $M$-th mistake. Two quantities race.

\emph{Progress along $u$ grows linearly.} Each mistake adds
$y_t u^\top x_t \ge \gamma$ to $u^\top w$:
\begin{equation*}
  u^\top w \;=\; \sum_{\text{mistakes}} y_t\, u^\top x_t \;\ge\; M\gamma .
\end{equation*}

\emph{The norm grows at most linearly.} On a mistake,
$y_t w^\top x_t \le 0$, so
\begin{equation*}
  \lVert w + y_t x_t\rVert^2
  = \lVert w\rVert^2 + 2 y_t w^\top x_t + \lVert x_t\rVert^2
  \le \lVert w\rVert^2 + R^2 ,
\end{equation*}
hence $\lVert w\rVert^2 \le M R^2$ after $M$ mistakes.

\emph{Cauchy--Schwarz squeezes.} Since $\lVert u\rVert = 1$,
\begin{equation*}
  M\gamma \;\le\; u^\top w \;\le\; \lVert w\rVert \;\le\; \sqrt{M}\,R
  \quad\Longrightarrow\quad
  \sqrt{M} \le R/\gamma
  \quad\Longrightarrow\quad
  M \le (R/\gamma)^2 . \qedhere
\end{equation*}
\end{proof}

Read it: the bound involves \emph{only geometry} --- margin and radius
--- not the dimension, not the number of examples, not any probability.
A million points or ten, random order or adversarial: at most
$(R/\gamma)^2$ corrections. The script's separable run makes a few dozen
mistakes against a bound of a few hundred, then completes a clean pass.

\begin{remark}[Non-separable data: the failure mode]
If no margin exists, the theorem's premise fails and so does the
algorithm's behavior: updates never cease (the script's noisy run shows
mistakes accumulating linearly, forever). The perceptron has no notion of
``good enough'' --- every mistake triggers the same full-strength
correction, including on hopeless (mislabeled) points. Handling
non-separability by \emph{bounding} the correction is exactly the
Passive-Aggressive family; handling it by \emph{trading} margin against
slack is the SVM. Both next.
\end{remark}

\section{Multiclass perceptron}

One weight vector per class, prediction by best score:
$\hat y = \arg\max_k w_k^\top x$. On a mistake, promote the truth and
demote the pretender:
\begin{equation}
  w_{y} \leftarrow w_{y} + x,
  \qquad
  w_{\hat y} \leftarrow w_{\hat y} - x .
  \label{eq:multiupdate}
\end{equation}
The binary case is recovered with $w = w_{+1} - w_{-1}$ (the two updates
collapse into \eqref{eq:update}), and a Novikoff-type bound holds with
the multiclass margin
$\gamma = \min_t \bigl(w^\ast_{y_t} - \max_{k\ne y_t} w^\ast_k\bigr)^\top
x_t$ playing the same role. The same promote/demote shape is softmax
regression's gradient $(p - e_y)\otimes x$ hardened: probabilities
replaced by a single all-or-nothing correction --- the perceptron is the
zero-temperature limit of the statistical model it abandoned.
