\begin{abstract}
The perceptron corrects every mistake by the same fixed amount. The
Passive-Aggressive family (Crammer, Dekel, Keshet, Shalev-Shwartz \&
Singer, 2006) instead \emph{solves a tiny constrained optimization at
every step}: stay as close as possible to the current weights while
satisfying the incoming example's margin constraint. The KKT machinery of
the optimization note delivers a closed-form step size, and two damped
variants (PA-I, PA-II) buy noise robustness. Binary and multiclass
versions, with the closed form verified against brute force and the
noise-robustness race run in \texttt{pa.py}.
\end{abstract}

\section{The idea: passive when satisfied, aggressive when not}

Labels $y \in \{-1,+1\}$; margin target $1$ via the hinge loss
$\ell_t(w) = \max\bigl(0,\; 1 - y_t\, w^\top x_t\bigr)$. On each example
solve
\begin{equation}
  w_{t+1} \;=\; \arg\min_{w}\; \tfrac12\lVert w - w_t\rVert^2
  \quad\text{s.t.}\quad \ell_t(w) = 0 .
  \label{eq:paproblem}
\end{equation}
Two regimes by construction. \emph{Passive}: if $\ell_t(w_t) = 0$ the
current weights are feasible and optimal --- no update, unlike SGD, which
nudges on every sample. \emph{Aggressive}: otherwise, make the minimal
change that fully satisfies the example. The learner is a sequence of
projections onto half-spaces of ``examples I now respect.''

\section{The closed form via KKT}

\begin{proposition}
When $\ell_t(w_t) > 0$, the solution of \eqref{eq:paproblem} is
\begin{equation}
  \boxed{\;w_{t+1} \;=\; w_t + \tau_t\, y_t\, x_t,
  \qquad
  \tau_t \;=\; \frac{\ell_t(w_t)}{\lVert x_t\rVert^2}.\;}
  \label{eq:pastep}
\end{equation}
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
With the constraint active ($y_t w^\top x_t = 1$), the Lagrangian is
$\tfrac12\lVert w-w_t\rVert^2 + \tau\,(1 - y_t w^\top x_t)$ with
multiplier $\tau \ge 0$. Stationarity in $w$:
$w - w_t - \tau y_t x_t = 0$, so $w = w_t + \tau y_t x_t$ --- the update
direction is forced; only its length is free. Substitute into the active
constraint:
$y_t w_t^\top x_t + \tau \lVert x_t\rVert^2 = 1$, i.e.\
$\tau = (1 - y_t w_t^\top x_t)/\lVert x_t\rVert^2 =
\ell_t(w_t)/\lVert x_t\rVert^2 > 0$, consistent with dual feasibility;
complementary slackness holds since the constraint is met with equality.
\end{proof}

The perceptron's update is \eqref{eq:pastep} with $\tau$ hard-coded to
$1$; PA sizes the correction by how badly the example is violated,
normalized by its length --- large enough to fix it exactly, never
larger.

\section{Noise: PA-I and PA-II}

Aggressiveness is a liability under label noise: a mislabeled example
forces \eqref{eq:paproblem} to satisfy a \emph{wrong} constraint fully,
however far that drags $w$ (the script's race shows plain PA losing to
the perceptron on a noisy stream). The fix is to let the constraint be
violated at a price --- add a slack variable $\xi \ge 0$, exactly the
soft-margin move of the SVM note:
\begin{align}
  \text{PA-I:}\quad
  &\min_{w,\xi}\; \tfrac12\lVert w-w_t\rVert^2 + C\,\xi
  \;\;\text{s.t.}\; \ell_t(w) \le \xi
  &&\Longrightarrow\;
  \tau_t = \min\Bigl(C,\; \frac{\ell_t(w_t)}{\lVert x_t\rVert^2}\Bigr),
  \label{eq:pai}\\
  \text{PA-II:}\quad
  &\min_{w,\xi}\; \tfrac12\lVert w-w_t\rVert^2 + C\,\xi^2
  \;\;\text{s.t.}\; \ell_t(w) \le \xi
  &&\Longrightarrow\;
  \tau_t = \frac{\ell_t(w_t)}{\lVert x_t\rVert^2 + \tfrac{1}{2C}} ,
  \label{eq:paii}
\end{align}
(derivations: same Lagrangian with the extra $\xi$ term; the linear
penalty caps the multiplier at $C$, the quadratic one shrinks it).
$C$ is the trust budget per example: $C \to \infty$ recovers plain PA;
small $C$ means no single example --- honest or mislabeled --- can move
$w$ far. In the script's race (7\% flipped labels), PA-I with $C = 0.1$
recovers a clean-test error near the noise floor while plain PA thrashes.

\section{Multiclass PA}

Score with one vector per class; require the true class to beat the best
wrong class by margin 1. With
$\ell_t = \max\bigl(0,\, 1 - (w_{y_t} - w_{\hat k})^\top x_t\bigr)$ where
$\hat k$ is the highest-scoring wrong class, the same derivation (the
change now lives in two rows, whence the factor 2) gives
\begin{equation}
  w_{y_t} \mathrel{+}= \tau_t x_t,
  \qquad
  w_{\hat k} \mathrel{-}= \tau_t x_t,
  \qquad
  \tau_t = \min\Bigl(C,\; \frac{\ell_t}{2\lVert x_t\rVert^2}\Bigr).
  \label{eq:pamulti}
\end{equation}
Promote the truth, demote the pretender --- the multiclass perceptron's
shape with an optimized step, and one more instance of the ladder this
repository keeps climbing: softmax's soft $(p - e_y)$, the perceptron's
hard $\pm 1$, PA's ``exactly as hard as needed.''

\begin{remark}[Guarantees]
PA's analysis is regret-style, in the perceptron tradition: e.g.\ for
separable streams PA enjoys a $(R/\gamma)^2$-type mistake bound, and
PA-I/PA-II admit bounds that degrade gracefully with the cumulative
hinge loss of the best fixed predictor in hindsight --- competing with an
oracle, not estimating a distribution. Distribution-free to the end.
\end{remark}
