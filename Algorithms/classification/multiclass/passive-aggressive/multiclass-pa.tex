\begin{abstract}
Online multiclass learning as per-step constrained optimization
(Crammer et al., 2006): move the weights as little as possible while
satisfying the current example's margin constraint. The closed-form
update is derived via the KKT conditions and verified numerically:
the post-update margin is exactly one, the step is minimal-norm among
feasible moves, and the slack-capped variant (PA-I) dominates under
label noise. Companion program: \texttt{multiclass\_pa.py}.
\end{abstract}

\section{The per-step problem and its solution}

Weights $W \in \mathbb{R}^{K \times d}$; on example $(x, y)$ let
$r = \arg\max_{k \ne y} \langle W_k, x\rangle$ be the highest-scoring
wrong class and $\ell = \max\bigl(0,\, 1 - (\langle W_y, x\rangle -
\langle W_r, x\rangle)\bigr)$ the multiclass hinge loss. The
passive--aggressive step solves
\begin{equation}
  \min_{W'} \;\tfrac12\|W' - W\|_F^2
  \quad\text{s.t.}\quad
  \langle W'_y, x\rangle - \langle W'_r, x\rangle \;\ge\; 1.
  \label{eq:problem}
\end{equation}

\begin{theorem}[Closed form]
\label{thm:pa}
If $\ell = 0$ the solution is $W' = W$ (passive). Otherwise
\begin{equation}
  \boxed{\;\tau = \frac{\ell}{2\|x\|^2}, \qquad
  W'_y = W_y + \tau x, \qquad W'_r = W_r - \tau x,\;}
  \label{eq:step}
\end{equation}
all other rows unchanged, and the constraint holds with equality
(aggressive).
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The Lagrangian of \eqref{eq:problem} is
$\tfrac12\|W' - W\|_F^2 - \tau\bigl(\langle W'_y - W'_r, x\rangle
- 1\bigr)$ with multiplier $\tau \ge 0$. Stationarity in each row:
$W'_k = W_k$ for $k \notin \{y, r\}$, $W'_y = W_y + \tau x$,
$W'_r = W_r - \tau x$. When $\ell > 0$ the constraint is active
(complementary slackness with $\tau > 0$); substituting the
stationary rows,
\begin{equation*}
  \langle W_y + \tau x - W_r + \tau x,\; x\rangle = 1
  \;\Longleftrightarrow\;
  (1 - \ell) + 2\tau\|x\|^2 = 1
  \;\Longleftrightarrow\;
  \tau = \frac{\ell}{2\|x\|^2}.
\end{equation*}
The objective is strictly convex, so the KKT point is the unique
minimum. PA-I replaces $\tau$ by $\min(\tau, C)$: the solution of
\eqref{eq:problem} with a slack variable penalized at rate $C$,
trading exact margin satisfaction for robustness.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}
In Kesler coordinates (multiclass-perceptron.pdf) the step is the
binary PA update on the joint feature
$\Phi(x,y) - \Phi(x,r)$, whose squared norm is $2\|x\|^2$ ---
the denominator of \eqref{eq:step}.
\end{remark}
